# Fresh Nest Staff Portal — User Guide

This guide covers the Fresh Nest staff portal (FSM app) used by cleaners, leads and supervisors.

---

## Shift Board (`/shifts`)

The **Available Shifts** page lists open cleanings that you can claim.

### What each shift card shows
* **Service type**, e.g. Standard Clean or Deep Clean.
* **Estimated pay:** hourly rate × scheduled hours.
* **Date and time.**
* **Service area:** the town and the first three characters of the postal code, for example *Cornwall · K6H* or *Cornwall Island*.

The **full client address, name and phone number are not shown until you claim the shift.** This protects client privacy. After you claim a shift, open **My Jobs** to see the full address and client contact details.

### Shifts you won't see
* **Shifts during your blocked times.** If a shift overlaps a blocked window in your Profile (for example, a weekly appointment), it is left off your board completely. Other staff and clients never see your blocked-window labels.

### Shifts you can see but can't claim
The **Claim Shift** button is disabled, with an explanation, when:
* **Monthly earnings limit:** the shift would take you over the monthly limit set on your profile. The message shows by how much.
* **Travel time:** the shift doesn't leave enough travel time before or after a shift you already have that day. The message shows the buffer and the conflicting shift's times. Shifts in the same postal area as your other shift don't need the buffer, but they still can't overlap.

### Keeping the list current
The board refreshes automatically every minute and whenever you come back to the app. After you claim a shift, it disappears from the board and appears in **My Jobs**.

If a shift you tap was claimed by someone else a moment earlier, you'll get a message saying it has already been claimed.

If the board can't load (for example, when you're offline), you'll see a message asking you to check your connection and try again.
