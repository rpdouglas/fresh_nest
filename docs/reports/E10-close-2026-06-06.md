## Architecture Notes

- **Connector geometry:** `top-6` (24px) aligns the `h-px` line with the vertical centre of `h-12` (48px) step circles. `left-[12.5%] right-[12.5%]` spans from the centre of column 1 to the centre of column 4 in a 4-equal-column grid. `hidden lg:block` — only visible when 4 columns are in a single row; `aria-hidden="true"` marks it decorative.
- **`aria-hidden` on circles:** Step number `<div>` elements are `aria-hidden="true"` — the sequence is communicated by numbered `<h3>` headings read in DOM order. Screen readers hear "Book Online / Choose your service… We Confirm / You'll hear from us…" — ordinal meaning is clear without announcing the integer.
- **`rounded-full` exception:** Design system restricts `rounded` (4px) to brand buttons and cards. Decorative numbered UI elements (step circles, avatar shapes) are explicitly permitted to use `rounded-full`.
- **FAQ link target:** `/faq` route exists as a PlaceholderPage (E14 will build the full page). The link is live and navigates correctly today.
- **Background:** `bg-warm-white` alternates correctly after GalleryPreview's `bg-cream`. Homepage sequence now: `warm-white → cream → warm-white → cream → warm-white → cream → warm-white`.
- **`staggerChildren: 0.12`** — slightly slower than prior sections (0.1) to give the numbered reveal a more deliberate, instructional cadence.
