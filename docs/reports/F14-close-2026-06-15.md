## 6. Known Limitations (Documented — Non-Blocking)

| ID | Limitation | Impact | Path Forward |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01 | "Payroll by Role" pie chart groups all cost under `cleaner` — `payRateSnapshot` has no `role` field | Donut chart may appear sparse or single-colored | Join `assignedTo` UID to `staff` list to infer role at chart render time (low priority enhancement) |
| L02 | `averageJobDuration` falls back to scheduled window when `checkedInAt`/`completedAt` absent | Metric is approximate until F09 flow is exercised in production | Self-resolving as cleaners begin using the FSM portal |
| L03 | `/jobs` and `/staff` fetched client-side, date filtering done in JS | Acceptable at current scale (~50–200 jobs/month) | At 10k+ records: add Firestore composite index on `scheduledDate` + query server-side |
