# P3-E29 Close Report — Remove Public Pricing & Discounts (Quote Follow-Up)
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**ID:** P3-E29
**Spec:** [`docs/projects/P3-E29.md`](../projects/P3-E29.md) · **Plan:** [`docs/plans/P3-E29_PLAN.md`](../plans/P3-E29_PLAN.md)
**Strategy:** 2 — Quote-Request Flow (approved by the human 2026-09-30, with D1–D6 as recommended)
**Status:** ✅ Closed (goes live on merge to `main`; CI deploys the customer site and functions)

---

## 1. Summary

The public site no longer shows any price, price range, "starting from" figure, % or $ discount, referral reward or payment step. Customers send a **quote request**, and Fresh Nest follows up within 24 hours with a quote.

The site, the booking flow and the confirmation email and SMS (EN/FR) are reworded to match, so nothing promises a confirmed, priced booking. **Strategy 2 was chosen** because stripping prices while keeping "Your booking is confirmed!" (Strategy 1) would have misled customers.

## 5. Deviations from the Plan

1. **« Devis » instead of « soumission ».** The plan suggested « Demander une soumission ». The existing French site uses « devis » throughout, so « Demander un devis » was used for consistency.
2. **"Free" and "no extra charge" removed as well**, as a conservative reading of "any references to prices". "Get a Free Quote" becomes "Get a Quote", "free assessment" becomes "on-site assessment", and FAQ 9's "at no extra charge" is removed. "Deposit back" (a tenant's security deposit) and "100% eco" are not our prices and remain.
3. **Confirmation copy is status-aware.** It isn't a blanket rewording: admin phone-in bookings created as `confirmed` keep "Your booking is confirmed!", which is correct because the price was agreed on the call.
4. **No component rename.** `QuoteCalculator` was converted in place rather than renamed to `ServiceSelector`, to keep the diff small.
5. **`StepIndicator.tsx`, `Hero.tsx`, `Footer.tsx`, `LocationPage.tsx` and `ServicesOverview.tsx` weren't edited.** Their CTAs use shared i18n keys (`common.bookNow`, `nav.booking`, `locations.bookCta` and others), so rewording the keys updated them.
6. **Admin guide corrected beyond scope** (see §6.1).

## 6. Known Limitations and Follow-Ups

1. **⚠️ No message when a quote request is confirmed.**
   - `onBookingStatusConfirmed` creates the job but sends the client nothing.
   - So once a customer accepts a quote and Lauren sets the booking to `confirmed`, the customer gets no confirmation email or SMS. The admin guide previously claimed otherwise and has been corrected.
   - **Recommended next ticket:** send a "your cleaning is confirmed" email/SMS on the `pending → confirmed` transition. It could also carry the agreed price, which is P3-E26 territory.
2. **Referral codes are still generated.** `onBookingCreated` still writes a `referrals/{CODE}` document for every booking. Customers no longer see or redeem codes (D3 kept the collection untouched). Stop generating them when the referral programme is formally retired.
3. **Stripe is dormant, not removed.** The `createPaymentIntent` and `stripeWebhookHandler` functions, the `@stripe/*` customer dependencies and the Stripe CSP entries remain for future P3-E26 deposits. `onBookingStatusConfirmed` still captures a PaymentIntent if one exists, which only applies to older bookings.
4. **SEO:** `/pricing` now redirects. Search results pointing at price-intent pages or the unpublished blog post will lose ranking over time.
5. **Pre-existing, found during audit:** `PrivacyPage.tsx` uses `privacyPage.*` keys that don't exist in `en.json` or `fr.json`. **Confirmed:** there's no `privacyPage` namespace and no fallback text, so the live privacy page renders raw keys. That's a COMPLIANCE issue (PIPEDA/Law 25). **Fix as a priority ticket.** Also, several admin keys are missing (`admin.staff.exportModal.*`, `admin.payRates.errorLoading`, and others). Not changed here.
6. **A manual preview check is recommended before merge:** home, one service page, the Airbnb page, `/booking` (all 4 steps), `/thank-you` and `/pricing`, in EN and FR, at 375px and 768px.
