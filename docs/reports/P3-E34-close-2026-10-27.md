- `bookings.checklistTemplateId`.

Also fixed doc drift: §8 task shape is `labelEn` / `labelFr`, not `labelKey`.

There are **no `firestore.rules` or index changes.**
- Admins could already update jobs and bookings.
- Cleaners' writable job keys are unchanged.
- The emulator harness attaches a checklist under the real rules.

## Deviations and notes
- **Lock rule:** a checklist can change only while the job is `unassigned`, `assigned` or `acknowledged` **and** has no completions. Completions are stored by task ID.
- **Dispatch chip size:** the chip is smaller than 48px, to fit the fixed-height grid cards (it uses the same size as the service tag). The booking panel's **Change checklist** button is the full-size path; phone dispatch is phase 3 of the admin mobile review.
- **Harness seed:** templates now use `labelEn` / `labelFr`. They had the old `labelKey` shape, which the app never read.
- **Backfill source:** backfilled jobs get `checklistSource: 'auto'`. Nothing records whether their template was chosen by hand.
- **Staff guide:** unchanged. Cleaners see checklists exactly as before.
- **Not in this release:** cleaners or leads editing lists (needs a rules change), and composable add-on lists (Strategy 3).
