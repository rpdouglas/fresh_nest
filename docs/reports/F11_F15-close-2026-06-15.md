## 1. Executive Summary
Epics F11 and F15 deliver administrative compliance logging and automated scheduling alert infrastructure across the Fresh Nest Co. ecosystem:
- **Administrative Auditing (F11):** Implemented client-side security override tracking. When admins bypass blocks (such as blocked windows, travel buffers, or cleaner earnings limits), reasons are requested and logged. The new "Audit Logs" tab in the Customer Admin Panel provides Sarah with a searchable, filterable log of operations.
- **Staff Notifications & Alerts (F15):** Implemented real-time scheduling triggers via Firebase Cloud Functions. Staff receive immediate, localized SMS push notifications (via Twilio) when shifts are assigned, updated, or cancelled, and general shift board postings trigger in-app updates.
- **Notification Center:** A responsive Bell Icon dropdown with unread counts and a "Mark all as read" button was added to the FSM portal navigation bar.
