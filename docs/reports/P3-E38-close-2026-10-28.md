## PR 1 — Conventions, shared helpers, clock fixes

### What shipped
- **Shared helpers** in `packages/shared/src/domain/`, exported from `@freshnest/shared` and synced to `functions/src/shared/`:
  - **`time.ts`:** `BUSINESS_TZ`, `todayInZone`, `calendarDateOf`, `addDays`, `dayOfWeek`, `startOfWeek`, `startOfMonth` / `endOfMonth`, `monthKey`, `previousMonthKey`, `zonedParts`, `zonedWallTimeToInstant`, `elapsedMinutes`. Built on `Intl`, with no new dependency.
  - **`money.ts`:** `roundHalfUp`, `toCents`, `centsToDollars`, `multiplyCents`, `percentOfCents`, `HST_BASIS_POINTS`, `formatCents`.
  - **`scheduling.ts`:**
    - `estimateShiftPay` rounds to whole cents once;
    - `overlapsBlockedWindow` takes the day of week from the calendar date, not the device's zone.
- **Clock fixes** (each computed the UTC date, which is tomorrow after ~8 pm Eastern):
  - **Dispatch Board:** today, week range, day arrows, blocked-window day.
  - **Operations dashboard:** today/week/month/custom ranges. The client-side filter now compares Toronto dates directly; the old filter also misread custom dates as UTC midnight.
  - **Bookings filter defaults.**
  - **Customer "upcoming" page.**
  - **Quote form** minimum and maximum dates (F14, first half).
- **Scheduled Functions on Toronto time:**
  - **Reminders:** `0 9 * * *`, so they stay at 09:00 local after Nov 1 (owner decision).
  - **Earnings rollover:** `0 0 1 * *` local, with the archived month from `previousMonthKey(monthKey(now))`. Before, it ran on the **last evening** of the month (8 pm EDT / 7 pm EST).
  - **Renewals:** `0 2 * * *` local, with Toronto dates.
  - **Review emails:** hourly, in the Toronto zone.
- **De-duplication:** six private copies of the shift-duration calculation removed (FSM `MyJobsPage`, `JobPage`; admin `DispatchBoard`, `BookingDetailPanel`, `OperationsDashboard`, `useOperationsDashboard`). The FSM pay estimates use `estimateShiftPay`.
- **Guards:**
  - ESLint `no-restricted-syntax` in `apps/customer` and `apps/fsm` rejects `toISOString().split/slice/substring`. It found one more case, in `BookingPage.test.tsx`, now fixed.
  - `functions/test/noUtcDates.test.ts` scans `functions/src` for the same pattern.
- **Docs:**
  - ADR-020 (Accepted);
  - `firestore-schema.md`: date fields described as Toronto dates, plus a new §16 on dates, times and money;
  - spec, plan, `ACTIVE_CYCLE` (P3-E38 row, F14 partial, P3-E37 row corrected).

## PR 2 — Money to integer cents

### What shipped
- **Fields** (documented in `firestore-schema.md`):

  | Before (dollars) | Now (integer cents) |
  |---|---|
  | `bookings.estimatedPrice` | `estimatedPriceCents` |
  | `payRates.amount` | `amountCents` |
  | `jobs.payRateSnapshot.amount` | `payRateSnapshot.amountCents` |
  | `staff.financials.monthlyEarningsLimit` / `currentMonthEarnings` / `earningsHistory[].total` | `monthlyEarningsLimitCents` / `currentMonthEarningsCents` / `earningsHistory[].totalCents` |
  | `settings/policy`: `defaultOdspMonthlyLimit`, `earningsCautionThreshold`, `lateCancellationFeeDollars`, `lockoutFeeDollars` | `defaultOdspMonthlyLimitCents`, `earningsCautionThresholdCents`, `lateCancellationFeeCents`, `lockoutFeeCents` |

- **Reading old records:**
  - The shared normalizers (`centsOrLegacy`, `normalizeFinancials`, `amountCentsOf` in `domain/money.ts`) read dollar fields as cents.
  - The app converters (booking, job, staff, pay rate) and Cloud Functions read through them.
  - `normalizePolicySettings` maps the old policy keys.
  - So nothing breaks before the migration runs.
- **Writes are cents only.**
  - When the server or the office rewrites an earnings total, it deletes the old dollar total for that record: shift claim, monthly rollover, office reassignment, a staff member changing their own limit.
- **Shared helpers:** `estimateShiftPayCents`, `earningsOverageCents`.
- **Functions:**
  - **Claim:** `claimJob` returns `newEarningsCents`.
  - **Open shifts:** `listOpenShifts` returns `payRateCents`, `estimatedPayCents`, `overageCents`.
  - **Staff registration:** takes `monthlyEarningsLimitCents`.
  - **Payments:** `createPaymentIntent` takes `estimatedPriceCents` and adds HST with `percentOfCents(…, 1300)`. No app calls it today.
  - **Analytics:** sums in cents, still reports dollars to the charts, and buckets by Toronto month.
- **Admin screens:**
  - Pay Rates, Register staff, Settings & Policies and the earnings limit take dollars and save cents.
  - Pay Rates "effective from" dates now start at midnight Toronto time; `new Date('YYYY-MM-DD')` was UTC midnight, i.e. 8 pm the evening before.
  - Dispatch Board, booking panel, Operations dashboard and the staff export compute pay in cents.
  - Amounts show through `formatCents`: EN `$1,234.50`, FR `1 234,50 $`. The `$` was hard-coded in JSX and in three strings.
  - Audit History shows `…Cents` fields as currency.
- **Staff app:**
  - Shift Board, the Safe-to-Earn bar, Profile (own limit) and the job pay estimates use cents and `formatCents`.
  - The Profile caution threshold comes from `earningsCautionThresholdCents`.
- **Rules** (`firestore.rules`, `firestore.dev.rules`) — **merging the PR is the human approval, per ADR-018:**
  - **Office booking create:** `estimatedPriceCents is int`, replacing `estimatedPrice is number`.
  - **Staff self-update:** may not change `currentMonthEarningsCents`, `currentMonthEarnings` or `earningsHistory`. The old dollar key stays protected until the clean-up PR, because a staff member could otherwise lower it on a record the migration hasn't reached.
- **Migration:** `functions/scripts/migrate-money-to-cents.cjs`.
  - **Modes:** dry run by default; `--apply` writes.
  - **Safety:**
    - per-document "not changed since read" precondition;
    - change stamp `system:migrate-money-to-cents` on audited records (policy unstamped, no new version);
    - dollar fields kept;
    - idempotent.
- **`PERSONAS.md` v4.3** (owner-authorised 2026-10-28): the P7 and P12 field names use `…Cents`. Wording only.
- **Seeds:** the E2E and admin-screenshot seeds now write cents.

## PR 3 (`F23`) — Clean-Up of Legacy Dollar Fields & Fallbacks

### What shipped
- **`migrate-money-to-cents.cjs`:** Added `--strip-legacy` (only strips a legacy dollar field when its `…Cents` counterpart is already present on the document) and unit tests in `migrateMoneyToCents.test.ts`.
- **Shared domain & types (`packages/shared/src/`, synced to `functions/src/shared/`):**
  - Removed `centsOrLegacy` from `domain/money.ts`; `normalizeFinancials` and `amountCentsOf` read only integer `…Cents` fields.
  - Removed `LEGACY_DOLLAR_KEYS` fallback from `domain/policy.ts`.
  - Removed `@deprecated` `Booking.estimatedPrice`, `PayRateSnapshot.amount`, and `PayRate.amount` from shared types and `bookingConverter`.
- **Apps & Cloud Functions:**
  - Removed transitional `deleteField()` / `FieldValue.delete()` of old dollar fields in `apps/customer/src/lib/firebase/firestore.ts`, `apps/fsm/src/pages/ProfilePage.tsx`, and `functions/src/jobs.ts`.
  - Removed `'estimatedPrice'` select and fallback in `functions/src/callable/analytics.ts`.
  - Updated `dayOf` in `apps/customer/src/components/admin/audit/auditDisplay.ts` to use `calendarDateOf` (`America/Toronto`).
  - Removed unused `admin.settings.fields.{defaultOdspMonthlyLimit,earningsCautionThreshold,lateCancellationFeeDollars,lockoutFeeDollars}` in EN/FR locale files while retaining `admin.audit.fields.*` so historical 7-year `auditLog` rows render cleanly.
- **Security rules (`firestore.rules`, `firestore.dev.rules`) — merging the PR is human approval per ADR-018:**
  - Dropped `'currentMonthEarnings'` from the protected staff self-update list (`['currentMonthEarningsCents', 'earningsHistory']`).
- **Schema & cycle docs:** Removed deprecated dollar field rows from `firestore-schema.md` (v2.2); marked `P3-E38`, `F3`, `F22`, and `F23` ✅ Complete in `ACTIVE_CYCLE.md` and `HANDOFF.md`.
