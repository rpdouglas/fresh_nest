## Summary of What Shipped

Implemented the full **Strategy 3** Sales Pipeline across `packages/shared`, `functions/`, `firestore.rules`, `firestore.indexes.json`, `apps/customer` (Admin portal), and `apps/fsm` (Staff/Contractor portal):

1. **Shared Domain (`packages/shared/src/domain/pipeline.ts`):**
   - 6 stages (`new`, `contacted`, `site_visit`, `quoted`, `won`, `lost`), fixed lost reasons (`LOST_REASONS`), note kinds (`call`, `sms`, `email`, `note`), owner roles (`owner`, `manager`, `cleaner`, `lead`, `supervisor`, `contractor`).
   - DST-safe `America/Toronto` business-hours SLA engine (`Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00`, `Sat 09:00–15:00`, Sun closed) for 2h cleaning / 1h site-visit first-contact targets.
   - Priority sorting, recurring-renewal exclusion (`system:recurring-renewal`), address minimization (`extractLeadArea`), status↔stage mapping, and `computePipelineKpis`.
   - Added `pipeline` to `AUDIT_CONFIG.bookings.fields` (P3-E37), 3 new `settings/policy` keys (`defaultLeadOwnerEmail`, `leadFirstContactTargetMinutes`, `leadSiteVisitFirstContactTargetMinutes`), and `contractor` to `StaffRole`.
2. **Firestore Security Rules & Indexes:**
   - Updated `firestore.rules` and `firestore.dev.rules`: public intake forbidden from forging `pipeline`; append-only `bookings/{id}/leadNotes/{noteId}` readable/creatable by `isOffice()` only (no updates/deletes); direct `bookings` reads remain office + customer-own-read.
   - Added composite indexes (`pipeline.ownerEmail` + `createdAt`, `pipeline.stage` + `createdAt`).
   - Added emulator rules tests in `apps/customer/test/firestore-rules.test.ts`.
3. **Cloud Functions (`functions/`):**
   - `triggers/booking.ts`: initializes `pipeline` on non-renewal creates, routes EN/FR alerts to assigned owner (with `OWNER_EMAIL`/`OWNER_PHONE` fallback), syncs `status` ↔ `pipeline.stage` on confirm/cancel, and guards `onBookingCancelled` from sending `"cancelled by the customer"` SMS on Lost or office/staff cancellations.
   - `triggers/pipeline.ts`: `onBookingPipelineUpdated` alerts new owners on reassignment (email + SMS + in-app `lead_assigned` for staff/contractors) and suppresses self-assignment alerts.
   - `callable/auth.ts`: added `updateOfficeContact` so Owners can edit phone/language on any office member (including self) and Managers can edit their own.
   - `callable/leads.ts`: `listMyLeads`, `getMyLead`, `updateMyLead`, `addMyLeadNote` — `databaseId`-aware, Zod-validated, strict owner isolation, address minimized unless `needsSiteVisit || stage === 'site_visit'`, `won` blocked for non-office, `firstContactAt` auto-stamped on first call/sms/email note or leaving `new`, `lastChange` stamped with `source: 'staff_app'`.
   - `callable/staff.ts`, `callable/shifts.ts`, `jobs.ts`, `notifications.ts`: `contractor` role registration + server-side exclusion from Shift Board, claiming shifts, shift notifications, and monthly earnings rollover.
   - `callable/analytics.ts`: added `pipeline` KPIs to `getAnalyticsKPIs`.
   - `scheduled/leadDigest.ts`: `onDailyLeadDigest` at `08:00 America/Toronto` emailing due/overdue leads per owner in EN/FR.
   - `scripts/backfill-booking-pipeline.cjs`: idempotent dry-run / `--apply` backfill script.
4. **Admin Portal (`apps/customer/`):**
   - `PipelineTab.tsx` + `LeadDetailPanel.tsx`: My leads / Unassigned / All views, Priority List + accessible desktop Board View (keyboard stage dropdowns, no drag-only actions), 2-click inline owner reassignment, Airbnb & Site-visit filter chips, overdue badges with icon + text, required lost reason, internal quote input (cents), append-only Contact Log, P3-E37 `RecordHistory`, and quick **"Create E-Agreement"** button prefilled from the booking.
   - `TeamRolesTab.tsx` (office alert phone & language), `SettingsTab.tsx` (Section 5 Sales Pipeline), `AnalyticsDashboard.tsx` (Sales Pipeline KPI card), `RegisterStaffModal.tsx` (`contractor` option).
   - Full EN + FR i18n (`noPricing.test.ts` verified).
5. **FSM Staff/Contractor Portal (`apps/fsm/`):**
   - `MyLeadsPage.tsx` + `LeadPage.tsx`: mobile-first, 48px targets, 16px+ text, icon-first (P10), full EN/FR (P11/P14), tappable `tel:` link, stage updates (excluding office-only `won`), required lost reason, contact notes.
   - `FsmLayout.tsx`, `App.tsx`, `ProfilePage.tsx`: `contractor` sees My Leads & Profile only (shifts/jobs/earnings hidden and redirected); other staff see My Leads when assigned leads exist; `lead_assigned` notifications link straight to `/leads/:id`.
