  "ariaLabel": "Trust and quality indicators",
  "insured":    "Insured & Bonded",
  "background": "Background-Checked Staff",
  "eco":        "Eco-Friendly Products",
  "guarantee":  "Satisfaction Guarantee",
  "rating":     "4.9 / 5 Google Rating",
  "bilingual":  "Bilingual Service"

French uses comma decimal for rating: `"4,9 / 5 Note Google"`. Keys are semantically independent from `footer.insured` / `footer.bilingual` (different copy form).

## Design Notes

- `bg-cream` / `border-y border-sand` — compact strip visually separated from hero's `bg-warm-white`
- `py-4 md:py-6` — trust bar stays above the fold on desktop alongside the hero
- `flex flex-wrap justify-center` — 6 items single-row on desktop, wraps cleanly on mobile
- Inline SVG icons (`aria-hidden="true"`) — no emoji, no external icon library
- Framer Motion `delayChildren: 0.7` — sequences strip animation after hero settles
- Eco item (`/services`) — entire badge is the touch target with `min-h-[48px] md:min-h-0`
