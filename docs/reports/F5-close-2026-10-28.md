## What changed
- **Harness (`apps/customer/e2e/harness/`):**
  - `swapFirebase.ts`: a Vite plugin that serves the real app with its Firebase module swapped. Also now used by the admin screenshot suite.
  - `customer-firebase.ts`, `fsm-firebase.ts`: emulator-connected Firebase modules.
  - `vite.customer.config.ts` (:5190), `vite.fsm.config.ts` (:5191).
  - `seed.cjs`: Mike (staff, current terms 2.1), two copies of his job, and a customer account.
  - `emulator.ts`: reads and resets documents past the rules.
- **Config:**
  - `firebase.e2e.json`: Auth, Firestore and Storage emulators.
  - `e2e/run.sh`: `npm run test:e2e` runs inside `emulators:exec`.
  - `playwright.config.ts`: Chromium only, `retries: 0`.
- **Specs:**
  - Rewritten to match the quote-first UI.
  - No `page.route` Firebase fakes and no fake JWT.
  - The axe audits wait until the page has finished rendering.
  - New tests: photo upload, customer refusal, and the saved-booking check.
- **CI (ADR-019):**
  - New `e2e` job in `firebase-preview.yml` for customer, FSM, shared or rules changes. A failure shows red, but it is **not required** during the trial.
  - `e2e.yml` (weekly) moved to the emulators and no longer uses Firebase secrets.
  - Traces are uploaded when a run fails.
- **Bugs the suite exposed:**
  - **FSM sign-in always denied, and the refusal message erased:** fixed in HOTFIX-11, a separate PR.
  - **Footer contrast:** `text-text-muted` on `bg-charcoal` was 2.3:1, failing WCAG AA. Footer text and links now use `text-sand` (8.8:1); the owner chose this on 2026-10-28. `design-system.md` contrast table updated.
- **Housekeeping:** `test-results/` is now ignored, and the stray tracked `.last-run.json` was removed.
