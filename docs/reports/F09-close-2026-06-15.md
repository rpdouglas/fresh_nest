## 1. Executive Summary
Epic F09 delivers the job execution flow for staff cleaners on the Fresh Nest FSM Portal. It features:
- Complete offline-first execution with local caching for check-in/out and checklist tracking.
- Dynamic visual, icon-first task items for ESL cleaners.
- Two-tap checklist completion verification preventing accidental updates.
- Geotagging and timestamp auditing.
- Offline camera upload queueing via a native IndexedDB wrapper and background sync context.
- Full Arabic language support, automatic RTL layouts, and a first-login language configuration gate.
