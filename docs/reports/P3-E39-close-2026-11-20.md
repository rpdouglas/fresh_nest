## Owner Decisions Applied (2026-11-20)
1. **Strategy 2** — append-only `agreements/{id}/signatures/{sigId}`, immutable-once-published `agreementTemplates/{id}/versions/{v}`, `party{kind:'booking', ...}`, frozen `terms` from `settings/policy`, restricted `agreements/{id}/private/access`.
2. **Narrow P3-E29 pricing exception** — `/agreement/:token` (`noindex`) and signed PDFs display that client's agreed rate and fees from Firestore; `'agreement.'` excluded in `apps/customer/src/i18n/noPricing.test.ts`; documented in `docs/COMPLIANCE.md`.
3. **Roles & access codes** — Owner and Manager (`isOffice()`, ADR-017) manage agreements and reveal restricted access codes in Admin; crew visibility deferred to Wave 1 Sites.
4. **Retention & deletion** — 7-year legal retention (`settings/policy.agreementRetentionYears`); added Owner-only, audited `redactAgreementClientData` callable and Admin **Clear Access & Answers** action.
5. **Legal business name** — `Fresh Nest Co.`.
6. **Link validity** — Default 30 days with Admin selector (`7`, `14`, `30`, `60` days).
7. **Link delivery** — Raw link returned once on create/reissue for copy/SMS, plus optional `"Email signing link to client now"` toggle via Resend in the client's language (`Reply-To: hr.freshnest@gmail.com`).
8. **Rollout** — New clients + changed service first, then existing active clients in manual batches.
9. **Structured terms & taxes** — `rateType` (`hourly | flat | monthly`), `taxExempt` toggle (respecting Akwesasne P4 / Snye QC P5 / Ontario), `latePaymentBasisPoints = 200` (2%), `noticePeriodDays = 14` (residential) / `30` (commercial), all editable prior to creation.
10. **DB routing & App Check** — Allowlisted `databaseId` (`'(default)' | 'freshnest-dev'`) on callables; dev PDFs saved under `agreements-dev/{id}/signed.pdf`; `enforceAppCheck` enabled in production (`process.env.FUNCTIONS_EMULATOR !== 'true'`).
11. **Templates** — Bilingual EN/FR placeholder templates (Residential & Commercial, v1) via `functions/scripts/seed-agreement-template.cjs`.
