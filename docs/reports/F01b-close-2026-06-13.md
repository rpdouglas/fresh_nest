## 2. Technical Strategy & Implementation Details

1. **Security Rules Implementation:**
   - Evaluated the access requirements for FSM collections:
     - `staff`: Only the staff member themselves can read their profile; write/admin access is restricted to admins.
     - `jobs`: Assigned cleaners can read, and update specific fields: `['status', 'checkedInAt', 'checkedInGeo', 'completedAt', 'checklistCompletions', 'photos']`. Admins can read, write, and delete.
     - `payRates` and `checklistTemplates`: Only authenticated staff can read, and admins can write.
     - `auditLog`: Read-only for admins, write-disabled for all clients.
   - Replaced the catch-all `match /{document=**}` rules with these specific paths, ensuring the default-deny rule is the last rule in both files.
   - Fixed an inconsistency where the `referrals` collection was missing from the production `firestore.rules`.
2. **Content-Security-Policy (CSP) configuration:**
   - Modified `firebase.json`'s CSP directive for the FSM target to permit connections and image loading from `https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com` and `blob:` (required for uploading and previewing task/job images).
3. **CI/CD Pipeline Upgrades:**
   - Configured `actions-hosting-deploy` to run in two sequential steps for production deployments (deploying `freshnest-prod` and `freshnest-fsm` to their live channels).
   - Configured the pull-request preview workflow to deploy both `freshnest-dev` and `freshnest-fsm` targets.
