## Known Limitation — Worth a Future Hardening Pass
`completeTrainingModule` trusts the client's assertion that the comprehension check passed — it does not independently verify quiz answers server-side. This matches the codebase's existing self-attestation trust model (`TermsConsentOverlay`'s checkbox and `EmploymentAgreementScreen`'s signature aren't server-verified either) and the original narrative spec's own client-side-quiz design. Since Module 4 (WHMIS) gates dispatch assignment, a technically sophisticated employee could theoretically call the Cloud Function directly with `moduleId: 'module4Whmis'` without passing the quiz. Not a regression this epic introduces, but worth a future pass — e.g. having the client submit its answers to the callable and letting the callable verify correctness itself — if WHMIS compliance needs a stronger guarantee than self-attestation.
