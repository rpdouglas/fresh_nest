# P3-E35 Report — Roles: Owner and Manager

**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Strategy:** 2 (rename with a transition alias), approved 2026-10-27 · **Status:** ✅ Code complete. **Rollout steps 2–4 are pending (human)**, then a cleanup PR
**Spec:** [`../projects/P3-E35.md`](../projects/P3-E35.md) · **Plan:** [`../plans/P3-E35_PLAN.md`](../plans/P3-E35_PLAN.md) · **ADR:** [ADR-017](../decisions/ADR-017-owner-manager-office-roles.md) (accepted 2026-10-27)

## Personas
- **P12 Lauren:** owns the company, so she is an **Owner**: the full admin panel plus Team & roles, where she controls office access.
- **Future office staff:** get the Manager role.
- **P7–P15:** unchanged.

## What shipped
- **Functions:**
  - **`lib/roles.ts`:** `normalizeRole` (admin → owner during the transition), `isOfficeRole`, `isOwnerRole`, `allowlistRole`, `checkRoleChange` (pure), `resolveOfficeRole` / `requireOffice` (claim first, then allowlist).
  - **`setUserRole`:**
    - office callers may set staff roles; only Owners may grant or revoke owner and manager;
    - nobody changes their own role, and the never-downgrade guard is kept (`forceDowngrade`);
    - removing someone from the office keeps the staff app if they're on staff;
    - **pre-approval:** for an email with no account yet, an Owner can grant, switch or withdraw Owner/Manager. Only the allowlist changes (the audit entry has `collection: 'admins'`), and `onUserCreated` applies the role on first sign-in. Staff roles still need an account;
    - it keeps the `admins/{email}` allowlist in step and writes an `auditLog` entry for each change.
  - **Office gates:** `onStaffRegistered`, `resendWelcomeEmail`, `migrateComplianceRecords` and `getAnalyticsKPIs` use `requireOffice` / `resolveOfficeRole`. `listOpenShifts` admits office roles.
  - **`onUserCreated`:** assigns the allowlist role (owner or manager) and uses the shared priority table.
- **Rules:**
  - `firestore.rules` and `firestore.dev.rules`: `isAdmin()` becomes `isOffice()` (owner, manager, legacy admin); `isStaff()` includes office roles.
  - The `admins` collection is readable by the office (Team & roles list), and writes are still denied.
  - `storage.rules`: office roles for the gallery and crew-photo reads.
- **Apps:**
  - `@freshnest/shared` gains `roles.ts` (`officeRoleOf`, `isOfficeRole`, `isStaffAppRole`);
  - `useAdminAuth` returns `officeRole` and `isOwner`;
  - the customer and staff route guards and `StaffAuthProvider` use the helpers.
  - **Team & roles tab** (Owner-only, EN/FR): list (Owners first), give access by email, Make Owner / Make Manager, Remove access (with a confirmation), and plain messages, including "pre-approved — they get access the first time they sign in".
- **Migration:** `functions/scripts/migrate-admin-to-owner.cjs` (dry run by default) changes `admin` claims to `owner` and fills in `role: owner` on legacy allowlist entries.

## Rollout (human), in this order
1. **Merge** (functions and apps deploy). The office keeps working because `admin` is still accepted.
2. **Deploy rules:** Actions → **Deploy Security Rules**, target `all` (Firestore rules, Storage rules and indexes), and type DEPLOY.
3. **Run the migration** from `functions/`, a dry run first:
   `node scripts/migrate-admin-to-owner.cjs --database='(default)'`, then `--apply`.
4. **Sign out and in again** on the admin panel. The **Team & roles** tab appears for Owners. Lauren is an Owner: either she was given access before the merge (see below) and the migration made her an Owner, or an existing Owner adds her as **Owner** here. This works even if she has never signed in (pre-approval).
5. **Then, the cleanup PR:** remove `'admin'` from the rules, `normalizeRole` and `officeRoleOf`, and add tests that `admin` no longer passes.
