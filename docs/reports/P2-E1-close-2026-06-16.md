## 1. Summary of Changes

- **Magic-Link & Google Sign-In Authentication**: Created [CustomerAuthContext.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/components/layout/CustomerAuthContext.tsx) and [CustomerProtectedRoute.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/components/layout/CustomerProtectedRoute.tsx) to provide passwordless magic link email authentication and Google login as a secondary provider.
- **Customer Portal Views & Layout**: Created [CustomerPortalLayout.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/components/layout/CustomerPortalLayout.tsx) layout component and several pages inside `apps/customer/src/pages/customer/`:
  - [LoginPage.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/pages/customer/LoginPage.tsx): Passwordless request screen.
  - [LoginConfirmPage.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/pages/customer/LoginConfirmPage.tsx): Email link verification receiver.
  - [CustomerBookingsPage.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/pages/customer/CustomerBookingsPage.tsx): Customer booking history and re-booking.
  - [CustomerUpcomingPage.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/pages/customer/CustomerUpcomingPage.tsx): Customer upcoming schedule and self-cancellation.
  - [CustomerProfilePage.tsx](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/apps/customer/src/pages/customer/CustomerProfilePage.tsx): Customer profile details form (firstName, lastName, phone, address).
- **Asynchronous Custom Claims**: Created the 1st Gen Firebase Auth `onUserCreated` trigger in [functions/src/index.ts](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/functions/src/index.ts) to automatically assign the `{ role: 'customer' }` custom claim and create a Firestore `/customers/{uid}` profile document on new registrations without requiring Identity Platform (GCIP).
- **Booking Cancellation Trigger**: Added `onBookingCancelled` Firestore trigger in [functions/src/index.ts](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/functions/src/index.ts) to handle automated cancellation logic:
  - Stripe payment hold release via the Stripe API.
  - Updating associated jobs status to `cancelled` and setting `cancelledAt`.
  - Twilio SMS notification sent to the owner/admin.
- **Firestore Security Rules**: Secured bookings and customer profiles in both [firestore.rules](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/firestore.rules) and [firestore.dev.rules](file:///workspaces/fresh_nest/firestore.dev.rules), allowing reads only when `request.auth.token.email == resource.data.email`, and permitting cancellations specifically by limiting updates to the `status` field.
- **Localization & Copy**: Fully localized all newly introduced screens in French and English in `en.json` and `fr.json`.
