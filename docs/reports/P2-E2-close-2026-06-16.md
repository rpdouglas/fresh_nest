## 1. Overview
Epic **P2-E2** automates post-job client feedback loops to drive review volume. Exactly 24 hours after a cleaning job transitions to `completed`, the system dispatches a localized email (via Resend) linking the client directly to a zero-friction, pre-populated review page. Review submissions are saved in Firestore under a moderation queue, prompting highly satisfied clients (4-5 stars) to copy their review and share it on Google Business Profile. Admins moderate reviews via a new dashboard tab.

---

## 2. Work Done

### A. Firebase Cloud Functions
- **`onJobStatusCompleted`**: Firestore trigger on `jobs/{docId}` changes. When status becomes `'completed'`, it schedules email delivery by writing `reviewRequestScheduledFor: now + 24h` and `reviewEmailSent: false`.
- **`onReviewEmailScheduler`**: Hourly cron job querying jobs where `reviewRequestScheduledFor <= now` and `reviewEmailSent == false`. It pulls client email and language preference from the associated booking and dispatches a branded email via Resend.
- **Email Templates**: Built bilingual EN/FR transactional HTML email templates (`reviewRequestSubject` and `reviewRequestHtml` in `functions/src/emailTemplates.ts`) styled with the Fresh Nest design system color palette.

### B. Client Frontend Pages & Components
- **`/leave-review` Page (`LeaveReviewPage.tsx`)**: Secure review intake page pulling pre-populated reviewer details (name as first name + last initial, location as municipality name) from the booking. Supports full translation and changes the interface language to the customer's preference automatically.
- **Google Redirect Modal**: Intercepts 4- and 5-star submissions. It copies the review text to the client's clipboard and displays a modal linking to Google Business Profile for easy pasting.
- **`/reviews` Page (`ReviewsPage.tsx`)**: Unified public reviews page combining static testimonials and moderated (`approved == true`) dynamic reviews, ordered by date descending (displaying Month + Year).
- **Admin Moderation Tab (`ReviewsModerationTab.tsx`)**: Added to `/admin` dashboard. Displays real-time pending reviews with "Approve" (sets `approved: true`) and "Reject" (sets `rejected: true` to preserve the audit trail) buttons.

### C. Database Security & Performance
- **`firestore.rules` & `firestore.dev.rules`**: Added validated create permissions to `/reviews` collection allowing unauthenticated review creations for matching job IDs while restricting update/delete privileges to administrators.
- **Composite Indexes**: Added composite index for `jobs` on `reviewEmailSent` (ASC) and `reviewRequestScheduledFor` (ASC) in `firestore.indexes.json` to optimize the hourly scheduler query.
