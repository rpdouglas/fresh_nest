# Epic Close Report: E30 — Lead Source Dashboard
**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Author:** Dev Team

---

## 1. Executive Summary
We have successfully implemented the Marketing Analytics / Lead Source Dashboard (Epic E30) within the secure, protected `/admin` portal. By adopting Strategy 1 (Tabbed Admin Layout with Dynamic Recharts Dashboard), we successfully integrated full operational booking tracking with clean marketing performance visualizations without exposing new routes or endpoints.

---

## 2. Features Implemented
- **Tabbed Portal Interface:** Toggles between operational "Bookings Management" and marketing "Marketing Analytics".
- **Dynamic Date Filtering:** Built filters supporting *All Time*, *Last 30 Days*, *Last 90 Days*, *YTD*, and *This Month* to aggregate relevant historical data.
- **Lightweight KPI Metrics:** Dynamic counters estimating overall bookings volume, in-memory estimated revenues (leveraging standard bedroom/bathroom pricing rules), and average booking values.
- **Lead Source distribution (Pie Chart):** Responsive Donut chart visualization broken down by channels: `organic`, `google`, `referral`, `facebook`, `direct`.
- **Monthly Revenue & Booking Trends (Bar Chart):** Grouped chronology tracking month-over-month sales trends.
- **Bilingual Support (EN/FR):** Zero hardcoded strings. Utilizes react-i18next translations for all chart elements, table headers, and range options.
- **Clean Contrast & A11y Elements:** Complies with WCAG AA. Tappable targets (tabs, selects) are configured with `min-h-[48px]` to support mobile operators.
