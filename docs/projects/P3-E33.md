## Problem
The admin panel has no phone layout. The review measured these problems at 390px:
- **Public site shell:** `/admin` renders inside the public site's `Layout`.
  - The marketing footer adds about 1,000px.
  - The cookie banner covers the bottom 170px of every screen and every dialog.
- **Header:** takes about 290px before the tabs.
- **Tabs:** the 9-tab strip is 1,540px wide in a 358px space, with no sign that more tabs exist. Two tabs are off-screen even at 1280px.
- **Tab state:** the active tab isn't in the URL, so refresh or back always returns to Bookings.
- **Dialogs:**
  - they don't close on Escape, trap focus or lock page scroll;
  - `OverrideModal` can't scroll, so its Confirm button can fall off-screen.
- **Booking status:** a change, including to **Cancelled**, saves instantly with no confirmation. Cancelling cancels the job and texts the owner.
- **Copy bugs:**
  - Lead source shows the raw key `Admin.Leads.Organic`.
  - "Avg Job Duration" shows `0.0 0 hrs`.
  - Every analytics error is titled "Access Denied".
- **Markup bugs:**
  - Booking rows nest `<tr>` inside `<tr>`.
  - The detail row has `colSpan={5}` in a 6-column table.
  - `/admin` has two `<main>` landmarks.

- **P3 Margaret's accessibility rules** (48px targets, 16px text) apply to the admin UI under CLAUDE.md Rule 3.

## In scope (phase 1 only)
1. **Shell (phones):** hide the public Footer, CookieBanner and InstallPrompt on `/admin` below `md`.
2. **Header (phones):** compact H1 and a one-row profile with Sign Out.
3. **Navigation:**
   - a phone section switcher instead of the 9-tab strip;
   - the active tab in the URL (`?tab=`, all sizes; desktop looks the same).
4. **Dialogs:**
   - Escape closes;
   - focus is trapped and restored afterwards;
   - page scroll is locked;
   - `OverrideModal` scrolls;
   - full-screen on phones with a sticky action bar;
   - applies to `AdminBookingModal`, `RegisterStaffModal`, `ExportRecordModal` and `OverrideModal`.
5. **Confirmations:** before a booking status change to **Cancelled** or **Confirmed**.
6. **Bug fixes:** `admin.leads.*` strings (EN/FR), duration double-format, analytics error title, table row markup and `colSpan`, nested `<main>`.

## Out of scope (later phases of the review)
- Card lists for Bookings, Staff, Audit, Reviews and Pay Rates.
- Filter sheet.
- Booking sheet with Call, Text, Email and Map.
- Dispatch tap-to-assign agenda and touch drag.
- Analytics tiles.
- Gallery and Templates polish.
- Google sign-in redirect on mobile.

## Constraints
- **Desktop views (≥ 768px) must look the same** (human decision, 2026-10-27).
- No schema, `firestore.rules` or Cloud Functions changes.
- Every new string goes through `t()` in EN and FR. EN/FR only.
- Tailwind v3.4 design tokens from `docs/design-system.md` only. `rounded` (4px) for brand elements.

## Acceptance criteria / persona tests
- **P12 Lauren at 390×844:**
  - **First screen:** opening `/admin` shows the section switcher and the first Bookings content with no footer or cookie banner.
  - **Section switching:** she can switch to any of the 9 sections in **2 taps**.
  - **Refresh:** refreshing keeps the section.
  - **No sideways scroll:** no horizontal page scroll on any tab, measured at 360px. This excludes in-card table scrolling, which phase 2 replaces.
- **Cancel confirmation:** setting a booking to **Cancelled** asks first. Choosing "Keep booking" leaves the status, the job and the SMS untouched.
- **Dialogs:** every admin dialog closes on Escape and returns focus to the button that opened it. At 390×660, `OverrideModal`'s Confirm is reachable.
- **Copy and markup:** no raw i18n keys on any admin screen in EN or FR. "Avg Job Duration" reads like `2.5 hrs`.
- **Desktop:** 1280×900 screenshots of all 9 tabs are identical before and after, except the confirmation step, which only appears after a status change.
