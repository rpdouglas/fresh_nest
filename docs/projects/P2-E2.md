# Project Spec: P2-E2 Post-Job Review Automation

## Objective
Automatically email customers a review request 24 hours after a job is completed, collect feedback through an internal form, and prompt high-rating users to copy their reviews to Google Business Profile.

## Background
Acquiring reviews is critical for SEO rankings and customer trust. A fully automated post-job review request system ensures that positive experiences are captured and leveraged on both the website and Google.

## Personas Served
- **P2 Travis McLeod & P3 Margaret Storey:** Rely on reviews and social proof when booking.
- **Business Operations:** Needs automated organic review velocity on Google.

## Key Tasks
- Create a Cloud Function trigger `onJobStatusCompleted`:
  - Runs when a job transitions to `completed`.
  - Writes `reviewRequestScheduledFor: Timestamp.now() + 24h` on the job document.
- Create an hourly cron Cloud Function:
  - Queries jobs where `reviewRequestScheduledFor <= now` and `reviewEmailSent != true`.
  - Dispatches branded EN/FR email via Resend with a link to the review form.
  - Marks `reviewEmailSent: true`.
- Build review submission page (`/leave-review?jobId=...`):
  - Form collects rating (1-5 stars) and review text.
  - If rating is 4 or 5 stars, display a modal: *"Thank you! Would you mind sharing this on Google?"* with a button linking to Google Business Profile.
  - Write review to `/reviews` collection with `approved: false`.
- Build admin dashboard moderation panel:
  - Lists pending reviews.
  - Allows admin to approve or reject reviews.
- Update `/reviews` page to render `approved == true` reviews from Firestore.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Email is automatically dispatched in the booking's language 24 hours after job completion.
- Reviews submitted are saved in Firestore with `approved: false`.
- Positively rated reviews show a direct redirect prompt to Google Business Profile.
- Approved reviews appear on `/reviews` within 60 seconds of admin approval.
