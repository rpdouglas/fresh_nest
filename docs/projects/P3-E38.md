**Unblocks:** W0-6 (server-checked schedule writes), W2-6 (pricing core: "cases A–F reproduce to the cent")

## Why
The master spec makes three rules non-negotiable for every later module:
- **One scheduling core.** The web apps and Cloud Functions run the same pure code.
- **Time.** Instants are stored in UTC with the site's zone (America/Toronto). Dates, buffers and recurrence use local wall time, so the **November 1, 2026** clock change never moves a 9:00 visit.
- **Money is integer cents,** with tax stored beside it.

Today the code follows none of these consistently. Before wave 1 adds clients, sites and pricing on top, the conventions have to be fixed in an ADR and the existing code brought in line, while production still holds only test data (owner decision, gap analysis §7).

### Time: "today" and month boundaries are computed in UTC
| Where | Code | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch Board "today", week range, day arrows | `toISOString().split('T')[0]` | After ~8 pm Eastern (7 pm after Nov 1) the board jumps to tomorrow |
| Operations dashboard ranges | same | Today/week/month figures shift in the evening |
| Bookings filter defaults (`useBookings.ts`), customer "upcoming" page | same | Same evening shift |
| Quote form minimum date (F14) | UTC tomorrow | After ~8 pm it skips a day |
| `onMonthlyEarningsRollover` | `0 0 1 * *` **UTC** | Runs at 8 pm (7 pm after Nov 1) on the **last day** of the month. Shifts in the month's last evening count toward the **next** month's ODSP/OSAP limit (P7 Carla, P13 Marcus) |
| `onDailyReminderCheck` | `0 13 * * *` UTC | Day-before reminders go out at 9 am now, **8 am after Nov 1** (P14 Sylvie) |
| `renewals` (02:00 UTC), `reviews` (hourly) | UTC dates | The renewal "today" is already tomorrow in Toronto |

Job times are local strings (`scheduledDate` `YYYY-MM-DD`, `scheduledStartTime`/`EndTime` `HH:MM`). This is *safe across Nov 1 by accident*: nothing converts them to instants, so no visit moves. The real Nov 1 effects are the cron times above.

### Money: decimal dollars everywhere, rounded only at Stripe
- **Where:**
  - `bookings.estimatedPrice`
  - `payRates.amount`, `jobs.payRateSnapshot.amount`
  - `staff.financials.{monthlyEarningsLimit, currentMonthEarnings, earningsHistory[].total}`
  - `settings/policy`: `defaultOdspMonthlyLimit`, `earningsCautionThreshold`, `lateCancellationFeeDollars`, `lockoutFeeDollars`
- **Earnings:** `estimateShiftPay` (rate × hours) is added to `currentMonthEarnings` as a floating-point number, then compared with the ODSP limit.
- **HST:** computed only in `createPaymentIntent` (`Math.round(price × 1.13 × 100)`).
- **Size:** about 35 source files and two rules lines (`firestore.rules` 147, 212) touch these fields.

## Acceptance criteria (all strategies)
1. **ADR-020 (Accepted):**
   - money in integer cents (field-name convention, rounding rule: round half up, once);
   - business zone `America/Toronto`;
   - calendar dates and wall times are local;
   - instants stored as UTC Timestamps;
   - every scheduled Function declares `timeZone: 'America/Toronto'`.
2. **One shared module of pure helpers** in `packages/shared/src/domain/`, synced to `functions/`, used by `apps/customer`, `apps/fsm` and `functions`:
   - **time:** `todayInZone`, `addDays`, `monthKey`, `zonedWallTimeToInstant`, …
   - **money:** `toCents`, `formatCents`, `roundHalfUp`, …
3. **No `toISOString()`-derived calendar dates remain** in app or functions code. A lint rule or test guards against new ones.
4. **Fixtures** for the Nov 1, 2026 fall-back and Mar 14, 2027 spring-forward; a month-end evening; a date-line evening (20:30 local); and HST rounding at the half-cent.
5. **Scheduled Functions run on Toronto time:**
   - the reminder runs at 09:00 local;
   - the earnings rollover runs at 00:00 local on the 1st, and its month key is the Toronto month.
6. **The FSM app's private duration copies are removed** in favour of the shared helper.
7. Further scope per the approved strategy (money migration, job instants).
