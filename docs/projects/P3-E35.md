## Owner decisions (2026-10-27)
These replace the earlier "Manager = schedule, no pay" answer (gap analysis §7 Q6).
1. **Two office roles: Owner and Manager.** A Manager has **the same access as an Owner, including pay**. The **only** thing reserved for Owners is **giving or taking away the Manager or Owner role**.
2. **Rename `admin` → `owner`** in claims, rules and code. Production holds only test data.
3. **Several Owners are allowed.** Any Owner can add or remove Owners and Managers, but can't remove their own Owner role, so there is always at least one Owner.
4. **Managers see earnings and pay**, so the earnings-cap check keeps working as it does today.
5. **Payroll role: later**, with timesheets (wave 3). It is named in the model but not built now.
6. `supervisor` stays the **field supervisor** in the staff app. It doesn't get office access.

## Role model after this epic
| Role (claim) | Who | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| `owner` | Business owner(s) | Everything a Manager can, **plus** grant or revoke `owner` and `manager` |
| `manager` | Office / operations (e.g. Lauren) | The whole admin panel and every office permission (bookings, dispatch, staff, pay rates, analytics, audit logs, reviews, gallery, rules-protected data), and registering staff. **Can't** grant or revoke `owner` or `manager` |
| `supervisor` | Field supervisor | Staff app (unchanged) |
| `staff` | Cleaners and leads | Staff app, own jobs (unchanged) |
| `customer` | Clients | Customer portal (unchanged) |
| `payroll` | — | **Not built yet** (wave 3) |

## In scope
1. **Claims:** `owner` and `manager` replace `admin`.
   - `setUserRole`: only Owners may grant or revoke `owner` or `manager`; Managers may still set `staff`, `supervisor` and `customer`.
   - No self-demotion for Owners; there must always be at least one Owner.
2. **Rules** (`firestore.rules`, `firestore.dev.rules`, `storage.rules`): every admin check becomes "office" (owner or manager). There are no Owner-only data paths.
3. **Functions** (`auth`, `staff`, `shifts`, `analytics` callables; `onUserCreated`; the staff trigger): role checks and the role-priority table updated. The `admins/{email}` allowlist carries a `role` field (`owner` or `manager`).
4. **Apps:**
   - `useAdminAuth` admits `owner` and `manager`;
   - the customer and staff route guards and `StaffAuthProvider` treat both as office;
   - a new **Team & roles** section, Owner-only, lists Owners and Managers and lets an Owner add or remove them.
5. **Migration:** a one-time, dry-run-first script switches existing `admin` claims and allowlist entries to `owner`.
6. **Docs:**
   - a new ADR (amends ADR-011's taxonomy; ADR-011 itself stays unchanged);
   - `firestore-schema.md` (`admins.role`);
   - the admin guide (Team & roles);
   - the close report.

## Out of scope
- **Payroll role.** It comes in wave 3.
- **Per-tab restrictions for Managers.** None: Managers see every tab.
- **Server-side scheduling checks.** That's W0-6.
- **Audit of role changes.** In scope only as a basic audit entry; full audit everywhere is W0-3.

## Acceptance criteria / persona tests
- **Manager (P12 Lauren):** signs into `/admin` and uses every tab, including Pay Rates and Audit Logs. Gets a clear "Only an Owner can do this" if she tries to make someone a Manager.
- **Owner:** opens **Team & roles**, adds a Manager by email, removes them, and can't remove their own Owner role.
- **Rules tests:**
  - `owner` and `manager` both pass every former admin check;
  - `admin` no longer does after cleanup;
  - `staff`, `supervisor` and `customer` are unchanged.
- **Functions tests:**
  - a Manager calling `setUserRole` with `manager` or `owner` is refused, while staff roles are allowed;
  - an Owner can't demote themselves.
- **No lockout during rollout:** the order of deploys never leaves the office without access (see the plan).
