# P3-E29 — Remove Public Pricing & Discounts (Quote Follow-Up)

**Type:** Customer-site content and flow change · **Complexity:** M · **Priority:** P1
**Opened:** 2026-09-30
**Relationship:** First slice of P3-E26 (Quote-First Booking System). Makes the whole public site quote-first. It does **not** build P3-E26's quote documents, signing, deposits or follow-up automation.

---

## Request (human, 2026-09-30)

> Review the customer site and remove any references to prices or discounts. Those will be discussed in a follow-up to a quote.

Prices and discounts appear in these places on the public site:

**Not affected (internal):** the admin dashboard (estimates, pay rates, analytics) and the FSM app.

**Confirmation emails and SMS contain no prices.** The client email says "Your booking is confirmed!", which will be wrong for a quote request.

## Scope

- **Remove:** every price amount, price range, "starting from", percentage and dollar discount, and referral reward from customer-facing pages, copy (EN + FR) and structured data.
- **Booking flow:** no payment is collected, because there's no price. Submitting creates a `pending` booking/request, and a human follows up with a quote.
- **Wording:** customer-facing copy and the confirmation email/SMS say a quote will follow, not that a price has been agreed.
- **Out of scope:**
  - P3-E26 quote documents, e-signing and deposits.
  - Admin-side estimates.
  - The FSM app.
  - Deleting Stripe Cloud Functions. They stay deployed and unused, ready for P3-E26 deposits.

## Acceptance criteria (Persona Tests)

1. **No prices anywhere public:** no `$` amount, price range, "starting from", % or $ discount, or referral reward on any customer-facing route, in EN or FR. This is verified by an automated scan of the locale files, the built `dist/` and the page source.
2. **P3 Margaret:** at 768px with 48px targets, she can request a clean using the keyboard alone. The confirmation page and email say a person will contact her with a quote. The phone number is visible.
3. **P1 Diane:** a French request produces a French confirmation that says a quote will follow.
4. **P2 Travis (revised):** a request can still be submitted on a 375px screen in under 3 minutes with no account. Price visibility is replaced by "quote follow-up".
5. **No card:** no Stripe payment element loads, and no PaymentIntent is created for a public request.
6. **`/pricing`** no longer shows prices, and old links still resolve. The route redirects or becomes a no-price page.
7. `npm run build`, `npm run lint`, and the customer unit tests pass.
