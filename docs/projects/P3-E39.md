## Why
Fresh Nest's service agreements are on paper (V2). Draft v3 of the Residential and Commercial Service Agreements is being finalised. The owner wants a client to open a private link, review **their own** agreement, sign it with a checkbox and a typed name, and have both the client and the office receive a PDF copy by email. Every signature must be stored as evidence.

The FSM master spec already plans an Agreements module for wave 1. This epic brings its **click-to-sign** option forward for client service agreements. Depending on the strategy chosen, it is either a standalone feature or the first slice of that module.

## Acceptance criteria (all strategies)
1. **Create.** An Owner or Manager creates a residential or commercial agreement in Admin → Agreements, prefilled from a booking or typed in, with language, services, frequency, start date and rate. They get a link `https://www.freshnestco.ca/agreement/<token>` to copy. The page opens in the agreement's language.
2. **Token.** At least 32 bytes from a cryptographic random source, base64url. **Only its SHA-256 hash is stored.** It expires after the agreed period (proposed 30 days) and stops working once signed. Wrong, expired, used and malformed tokens all show the same neutral "link not valid" page with the office phone number.
3. **Read.** The page loads only through a server callable, which returns only that agreement's display data and records `viewedAt` (first view) without the browser reading Firestore.
4. **Content.** The page shows the client's details, the Master Terms and the matching schedule (residential or commercial) from the **frozen, published version** in the agreement's language. Agreement wording lives in Firestore, not in code or `en.json`/`fr.json`. Page chrome (labels, buttons, errors) uses `t()`.
5. **Client answers.** Phone, email, entry method, pets, alarm (yes/no), special instructions and valuables to disclose. **Door codes, alarm codes and key locations go to a restricted access record**, never into the agreement document, PDF, email or any later response.
6. **Sign.** Three separate controls are required: "I agree to sign electronically" (consent to e-signature), "I have read and agree to this agreement", and typed full name. A honeypot field is present.
7. **Server sign.** In one transaction, `signAgreement` checks the token (hash match, not expired, not signed), validates the form with Zod, and stores the evidence:
   - typed name, server time, IP, user agent, language, version ID;
   - SHA-256 of the exact rendered agreement text.

   It then locks the record (status `signed`, token cleared).
8. **PDF.** Contains the agreement text, the client's details and an audit page with the evidence and the document hash. **No access codes.** It is stored in Storage under `agreements/`.
9. **Email.** Within 60 seconds, the client gets the PDF in the agreement's language, and `OWNER_EMAIL` gets the office copy. Both are sent from the verified Resend domain, with a Reply-To that receives mail.
10. **After signing.** The link shows "Signed, thank you" and cannot be signed again. A double submit or a replay returns the same result without a second signature.
11. **Abuse.** `enforceAppCheck` is on for the public callables. A per-agreement attempt limit and a honeypot are in place, and no PII is in the URL or logs.
12. **Rules.** Clients have no direct Firestore or Storage access to the new collections. Office read follows ADR-017, and writes are server-only. Emulator rules tests cover each path.
13. **Audit.** Create, send/reissue, view, sign and expire each write an `auditLog` entry (P3-E37 pattern).
14. **Docs.** The following are updated:
    - `firestore-schema.md` (new collections)
    - `COMPLIANCE.md` (with human approval: e-signature consent, access-code handling, the no-prices exception)
    - the privacy policy copy (IP and signing evidence)
    - `user-guide/admin-guide.md` (how to send an agreement)
    - ADR-021 (Accepted)

## Persona tests (Phase C pass/fail gates)
- **P3 Margaret:** At 768px portrait on an iPad, she opens a link and the phone number is visible without scrolling. All text is ≥16px and every control is ≥48px tall, with no horizontal scroll and AA contrast. She signs and sees the thank-you page.
- **P1 Diane / P5 Sophie:** A French agreement link opens with no English visible. The agreement text, the errors and the thank-you page are French. A French PDF and email arrive within 60 seconds. Sophie sees and accepts the evidence-consent wording.
- **P2 Travis:** Phone at 390px, from tapping the link to the thank-you page, takes under 3 minutes with no sign-in.
- **P12 Lauren:** She creates an agreement from an existing booking, copies the link, sees `viewed` after opening it on another device, then `signed`, and downloads the PDF. Reissuing gives a new link and the old one stops working. Each step appears in Audit Logs.
- **Security:** A reused, expired, wrong, or one-character-changed token is refused. No client-side Firestore read of `agreements` succeeds (rules test). No access code appears in the PDF, the email or any callable response after signing.

## Out of scope
Online payment and invoicing; automatic renewals and reminders for unsigned links (FSM spec G7 alerts); drawn signatures; syncing signed clients into scheduling (G3–G6 series activation); team/employee agreements; a "My agreements" view in the customer portal; a third-party e-signature service.

## Owner decisions needed (Phase A)
1. **Strategy** 1, 2 or 3 (see the plan). Recommendation: **2**.
2. **No-public-prices exception (P3-E29):** a private agreement page and its PDF may show that client's agreed rate and fees. Proposed: yes, limited to `/agreement/:token`, with the rate coming from the record and never from `en.json`/`fr.json`.
3. **Link validity:** 30 days proposed.
4. **Who creates agreements:** Owner and Manager (proposed, matching FSM spec Part 4 §1).
5. **Existing clients:** all sign, or only new clients and clients who change service.
6. **Legal business name** on the agreement and PDF ("Fresh Nest Co." assumed).
7. **Deletion versus retention:** signed agreements are kept for `agreementRetentionYears` (7) and a deletion request removes only the access record and the client answers. Proposed: yes.
8. **Access codes after signing:** visible to Owner/Manager only for now. Crew visibility comes with the wave 1 sites module.
